A social support app for injury recovery

## Project Description

RecoverTogether is a social media app that lets people recovering from injuries connect with others who have similar experiences. Users can share their recovery journeys, ask questions, discuss challenges, and support one another throughout the healing process.

The purpose of this application is to create a supportive online community where people can share personal experiences and find encouragement while recovering from injuries.

## Main Features

- **User Accounts:** Users can create accounts, log in, and manage their profiles.
- **Community Feed:** Users can create posts and view posts shared by other members.
- **Comments:** Users can comment on posts and offer support to others.
- **Injury Categories:** Users can explore posts related to different injuries, such as sprains, broken bones, and sports injuries.
- **Recovery Journal:** Users can record their recovery progress and personal experiences.
- **Post Management:** Users can create, read, edit, and delete their own posts.

## Project Objectives

The goal of RecoverTogether is to develop a social application that helps people connect and support one another during injury recovery. This project will allow us to practice software development, CRUD operations, teamwork, version control, and software testing.

The application will provide peer support and personal experiences, not replace professional medical advice.
