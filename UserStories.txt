RecoverTogether - 5 User Stories

User Story 1: Account Management
As a new member recovering from an injury,
I want to create an account and personalize my profile,
so that I can connect with people who understand what I am going through.

User Story 2: Community Interaction
As a college athlete recovering from a sports injury,
I want to share my recovery experiences,
so that I can connect with others who are facing similar challenges.

User Story 3: Sharing Advice and Encouragement
As a person who has recovered from a serious injury,
I want to comment on posts and share my experiences,
so that I can encourage others who are still going through recovery.

User Story 4: Finding Relevant Information
As a high school student recovering from a broken bone,
I want to explore posts about similar injuries,
so that I can learn how others handled their recovery process.

User Story 5: Tracking Recovery Progress
As a working adult recovering from surgery,
I want to keep a journal of my recovery progress,
so that I can track my improvements and reflect on the challenges I have overcome.
